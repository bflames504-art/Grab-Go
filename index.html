Our mission: make eating well the easiest choice on campus.

College makes healthy eating hard: no kitchen, no time, and no way to know what's in the food. Grab n' Go exists so you can get a real meal, know exactly what's in it, and be back to your day in minutes.

Why it matters

When you move into a dorm, you lose a lot of control over what you eat. There's no kitchen to cook in, and the choices are whatever is open when you get there.

Dining hall food usually comes without labels. If you're tracking protein or calories, you end up guessing at portions and hoping for the best.

Between classes, practice and work, there isn't much time left over. And with a meal plan already paid for, spending more somewhere else feels like a waste.

Grab n' Go

Grab n' Go is a student venture concept created by Kaitlyn Michael and Team 2 for an entrepreneurship course. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Coastal Carolina University or its dining services. This site is a demo: no orders are fulfilled and no payments are taken. Nutrition values are targets for concept recipes.

To plan our launch, this site records anonymous visits, clicks and demo order activity. Your email is collected only if you submit the reservation form, and names and room numbers never leave your device.
